# Recent ecosystem work and its relevance

Draft 0.3 | Source review 28 October 2026 | Prepared by edgepillar

[Overview](../README.md) | [Proposal](proposal.md) | [Architecture](architecture.md) | [Experiment plan](experiment-plan.md) | [Sources](../evidence/sources.md)

The wider ecosystem was considered in choosing this small dependency set. The entries below preserve the distinction between source, release, reported operation and activation. They are not a new independent audit of every project. Broad development activity is useful context; it is not evidence of customer demand for BTC checkout.

| Work considered | Implication for this proposal |
| --- | --- |

The current product can be designed around qualified existing fusion rules.

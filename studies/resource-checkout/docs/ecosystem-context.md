# Recent ecosystem work and its relevance

Draft 0.2 | Source observations through 28 October 2026 | Prepared by edgepillar

[Overview](../README.md) | [Proposal](proposal.md) | [Architecture](architecture.md) | [Experiment plan](experiment-plan.md) | [Sources](../evidence/sources.md)

The wider ecosystem was considered in choosing this small dependency set. The entries below preserve the distinction between source, release, reported operation and activation. They are not a new independent audit of every project. Broad development activity is useful context; it is not evidence of customer demand for BTC checkout.

| Work considered | Implication for this proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Core and activation | Release v0.0.9, Dynamic Plasma, libp2p spork definitions and community renewal are separate release and activation questions. The current product can be designed around qualified existing fusion rules. [S18](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/releases/tag/v0.0.9-alphanet) [S19](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/blob/dev/common/types/spork.go) [S20](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/pull/63) [S21](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/pull/101) |
| Recent integrity fixes | Receive recipient integrity and Pillar Update repricing improve specific paths. Respect non mainnet versus historical scope and do not reuse operator replacement policy for uncertain customer payments. [S22](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/pull/182) [S23](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/pull/183) |
| HTLC and PTLC | PTLC merged into dev; Ferry has an HTLC client and a developer reported swap. Tooling and recovery research exist. These address later asset exchange, not guaranteed service delivery or this initial BTC checkout. [S24](https://github.com/zenon-network/go-zenon/pull/138) [S25](https://github.com/sol-znn/ferry-web/blob/master/docs/SECURITY.md) [S26](https://forum.zenon.org/t/ferry-multi-chain-htlc-web-service/2366) [S27](https://github.com/sol-znn/ptlc-console) [S28](https://github.com/edgepillar/ptlc-research) |

Keep explicit operator trust for the first delivery observations. [S29](https://github.com/edgepillar/zenon-spv/blob/main/docs/verification-contract.md) [S30](https://github.com/edgepillar/zenon-spv/blob/main/docs/state-commitment-audit.md) [S31](https://github.com/digitalSloth/go-zenon/blob/feature/merkle-state-root/chain/nom/momentum.go) [S32](https://github.com/digitalSloth/go-zenon/blob/feature/merkle-state-root/docs/STATE_TREE_PREBUILD.md) |

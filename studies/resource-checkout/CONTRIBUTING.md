# Contributing to the research draft

This repository is an independent research proposal. It has no production implementation or operator commitment.

Useful review identifies a specific claim, its public primary source, the evidence level it supports and an observable correction or experiment. Challenge whether the service is needed, whether existing free and Lightning alternatives suffice, and whether the proposed measurements can distinguish customer benefit from payment substitution.

Keep repository text in English. Exclude real identity, private paths, credentials, wallet material, user payment records and private infrastructure details. Use pseudonymous attribution where appropriate. Do not copy upstream source or reports without checking their terms and retaining required attribution.

Treat source observations, reported tests, independently reproduced tests, integration, activation and production operation separately. Preserve failures and unknowns. Document checks do not qualify the proposed payment system.

Public availability does not imply submission to Spiral, a partnership or permission to speak for any cited project. Do not attribute commitments or reviews to people who have not made them. Before implementation, recheck current source overlap, exact revisions and the proposed operator boundary.
